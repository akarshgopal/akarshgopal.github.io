BlueHornet

Launchable GPS darts to reduce high speed pursuits.

In the demo, a chase car fires a GPS tracking dart from its grille at a suspect vehicle (100 km/h, 10 m). The dart attaches and starts flashing, and the police disengage and track the vehicle from a distance.

I handled systems engineering, software and finance: communication networks, state estimation and control, and computer-vision inference. The MVP was completed and validated.

ARCHIVE 01

Technical lead · from Sep 2019

A3T (Drone Team Twente): UAS Challenge 2020

Once again, I got bored and wanted to work on something hands-on, this time I was getting into robotics and drones. I joined the Uni’s newly minted drone team and participated in their first UAS Challenge entry. We had a team that was quite skilled but relatively inexperienced. We decided to build a VTOL quadplane with a carbon-fibre reinforced 3D printed airframe.

As technical lead I worked on the systems design, aerodynamic design and analysis, software-in-the-loop simulation of flight control, and integration and testing of the prototype. The airframe combined 3D printing with a carbon-fibre pultrusion space frame, and the UAV flew a Pixhawk 4 with the PX4 flight stack.

  • ModeVTOL · autonomous
  • Payload2.5 kg
  • Flight time27 min
  • Range32 km

ARCHIVE 04

Aug 2016 – Jan 2018 · Carbon Zero Challenge 2017 finalist

Vision X (Project X): an electric motorcycle

As a naive freshman in college, I was super bored with classes and was looking for something to work on hands-on. I was travelling to college every day on a scooter, and wanted to upgrade to a motorcycle. I wanted an electric one, but couldn’t find one.

I decided to make my own. I drew up a few sketches and specifications, and talked to a couple of friends about the project. They really liked it, and I realised other people might want one too, so I put together a team and started working on it.

I made the mistake of wanting to build all major components ourselves, but realised we were in way over our heads. I’m talking ground-up power electronics design like a 30 kW AC motor controller, a 6 kWh Li-ion battery management board, etc.

However, we managed to get support from the University as well as win some funds through a national competition: we made it to the finals of the Carbon Zero Challenge 2017 with a funding grant of Rs 5 lakh (about USD 7,000), and linked up with PES University’s research centre (CORI) to develop the powertrain. In the meanwhile the year was ending and an opportunity for me to study at a University abroad came up that I couldn’t pass up. So unfortunately I had to leave the project, but thankfully the team was still there! The team ended up building a nice prototype and got some recognition for it. They eventually established an EV club at the University.

  • Motor controller30 kW AC
  • Battery6 kWh Li-ion
  • GrantRs 5 lakh
  • ResultEV club
