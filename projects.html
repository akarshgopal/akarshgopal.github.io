BlueHornet

PDF Diff

TypeScript · React

Private PDF comparisons on your browser.

Compares two PDF revisions locally in the browser: a pixel overlay, side-by-side pages, a slider that reveals one revision under the other, and highlighted text changes. Also ships a headless CLI (@pdfdiff/cli) that emits overlays and JSON reports.

Branching AI chat: select any passage to explore it in a side branch, then bring the takeaway back.

Project X

As a naive freshman in college, I was super bored with classes and was looking for something to work on hands-on. I was travelling to college every day on a scooter, and wanted to upgrade to a motorcycle. I wanted an electric one, but couldn’t find one.

I decided to make my own. I drew up a few sketches and specifications, and talked to a couple of friends about the project. They really liked it, and I realised other people might want one too, so I put together a team and started working on it.

I made the mistake of wanting to build all major components ourselves, but realised we were in way over our heads. I’m talking ground-up power electronics design like a 30 kW AC motor controller, a 6 kWh Li-ion battery management board, etc.

However, we managed to get support from the University as well as win some funds through a national competition. In the meanwhile the year was ending and an opportunity for me to study at a University abroad came up that I couldn’t pass up. So unfortunately I had to leave the project, but thankfully the team was still there! The team ended up building a nice prototype and got some recognition for it. They eventually established an EV club at the University.
