What it is

Flowsheets with material, electricity, heat and consumable ports. A solver with capacity limits, bottlenecks, recycles and visible residuals. Economics: CAPEX, O&M, NPV and IRR after each solve.

A browser flowsheet workspace for connecting industrial process blocks (desalination, electrolysis, DAC, Sabatier, Haber–Bosch and more) and inspecting material, energy and economic flows.

It is built around one idea: that very cheap local solar DC, low-capex intermittent hardware and colocated conversion can matter more than chasing maximum utilisation or maximum electrical efficiency.

How it works

A deterministic operating solver runs the flowsheet with capacity limits, bottleneck reporting, recycles and visible balance residuals. A post-solve economics pass then folds in purchases, installed CAPEX, fixed and variable O&M, sales and replacements, and reports delivered cost, NPV and IRR. A location bar with a map binds typical-day solar data to a site. The numbers are screening estimates, not bankable figures, and the app says so.
