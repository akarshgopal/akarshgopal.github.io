Vision X: an electric motorcycle

An electric motorcycle, started as a freshman. Carbon Zero Challenge 2017 finalist.

Role
Started the project and the team
Period
Aug 2016 – Jan 2018
Recognition
Carbon Zero Challenge 2017 finalist
Partner
PES University research centre (CORI)
  • 30 kWAC motor controller
  • 6 kWhLi-ion battery management
  • Rs 5 lakhgrant, about USD 7,000

Story

As a naive freshman in college, I was super bored with classes and was looking for something to work on hands-on. I was travelling to college every day on a scooter, and wanted to upgrade to a motorcycle. I wanted an electric one, but couldn’t find one.

I decided to make my own. I drew up a few sketches and specifications, and talked to a couple of friends about the project. They really liked it, and I realised other people might want one too, so I put together a team and started working on it.

In over our heads

I made the mistake of wanting to build all major components ourselves, but realised we were in way over our heads. I’m talking ground-up power electronics design like a 30 kW AC motor controller, a 6 kWh Li-ion battery management board, etc.

What happened

However, we managed to get support from the University as well as win some funds through a national competition: we made it to the finals of the Carbon Zero Challenge 2017 with a funding grant of Rs 5 lakh (about USD 7,000), and linked up with PES University’s research centre (CORI) to develop the powertrain.

In the meanwhile the year was ending and an opportunity for me to study at a University abroad came up that I couldn’t pass up. So unfortunately I had to leave the project, but thankfully the team was still there! The team ended up building a nice prototype and got some recognition for it. They eventually established an EV club at the University.
