BlueHornet

Launchable GPS darts to reduce high speed pursuits.

What it is

A GPS tracking dart, fired from the grille of a police car, that sticks to the vehicle being chased. Once it is attached, the police can drop back and follow the vehicle from a distance instead of continuing a high-speed pursuit.

The demo

In the demo, a chase car fires a GPS tracking dart from its grille at a suspect vehicle (100 km/h, 10 m). The dart attaches and starts flashing, and the police disengage and track the vehicle from a distance.

What I did

I co-founded Myst Works B.V. to build BlueHornet. I handled systems engineering, software and finance: communication networks, state estimation and control, and computer-vision inference.
