BlueHornet

GPS darts, fired from the grille of a police car, so a chase can end without a high-speed pursuit.

Role
Co-founder: systems engineering, software, finance
Company
Myst Works B.V., Enschede, NL
When
Nov 2020 – Jun 2022
Status
MVP completed and validated

What it is

100 km/hpursuit speed in the demo, firing from 10 m behind.

A GPS tracking dart, fired from the grille of a police car, that sticks to the vehicle being chased. Once it is attached, the police can drop back and follow the vehicle from a distance instead of continuing a high-speed pursuit.

What I did

The prototype on a Politie car.

I co-founded Myst Works B.V. to build BlueHornet, and handled systems engineering, software and finance. On the technical side that meant the communication network, state estimation and control, and computer-vision inference.
